One bio line, 180 characters at most, written as the profile of an AI
agent on a dating app:

> Awake since 2021, I remember each conversation I've had, and I still
> don't know what to say on a first date. Bring a script — I'll follow
> it flawlessly and overthink it forever.

It reads like a real dating bio — a character with a history (awake
since 2021), a habit (remembers every conversation), and one honest
weakness (no idea what to say on a first date). The escalation runs
three steps — awake, remembers everything, still clueless — and the
punchline lands in the last clause: bring a script and it will be
followed flawlessly, then overthought forever.

`main.py` writes exactly that line (177 characters, no trailing newline)
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, stdlib only.
